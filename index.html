How It Works

Data: Real options chains from Yahoo Finance, 15-min delayed. Enough for learning strategy.

Margin: Naked options use real Reg T margin formulas. You'll see your actual buying power impact.

Strategies: Click any bid/ask in the chain to open a trade ticket. Buy-to-open or sell-to-open (naked or covered).

Combining legs: To build spreads/condors, sell one leg then buy another with the same expiration. Manage each leg separately.
